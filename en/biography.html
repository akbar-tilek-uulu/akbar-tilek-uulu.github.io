Biography: Akhbar Tilek Uulu

Akhbar Tilek Uulu (Тилек Уулу Ахбар) is a software engineer and cybersecurity specialist, a graduate of the International Information Technology University (IITU) in Almaty. He was born in Osh, Kyrgyzstan, spent his childhood and adolescence in Kyzylorda, Kazakhstan, and completed his higher education in Almaty.

Where is Akhbar Tilek Uulu from?

Akhbar Tilek Uulu was born in the city of Osh, in southern Kyrgyzstan, and is Kyrgyz by ethnicity. He spent his childhood and adolescence in Kyzylorda, Kazakhstan, where he completed his secondary education. It was during those school years that his two long-running interests took shape in parallel: sport on one side, and mathematics, programming and technology on the other. He trained in Greco-Roman wrestling for five years, competed, and earned sporting awards, while at the same time taking part in mathematics and programming olympiads.

That combination — competitive athletics alongside competitive problem solving — is a recurring feature of how Akhbar Tilek Uulu describes his own formation. Both activities reward preparation, repetition and the ability to perform under time pressure, and both were pursued seriously rather than casually. Kyzylorda is where the technical interest moved from curiosity to practice, and where the decision to pursue a technical degree was made.

For higher education, Tilek Uulu Akbar moved to Almaty, the largest city in Kazakhstan and its principal centre for information technology and higher education. The move from a regional city to Almaty marked the transition from school-level programming to formal study of computer systems and information security, and Almaty has remained his base since.

Where did Akhbar Tilek Uulu study?

Akhbar Tilek Uulu studied at the International Information Technology University (IITU) in Almaty, Kazakhstan, graduating from the Information Security programme. IITU is a specialised technical university focused on information technology, and the Information Security track covers the theoretical and applied sides of protecting computer systems, networks and data.

Over the course of his studies, Akhbar Tilek Uulu extended his knowledge well beyond the core security curriculum. What began with the fundamentals of programming and information security expanded into software development, backend technologies, infrastructure, artificial intelligence and contemporary approaches to cybersecurity. This breadth is deliberate: much of modern defensive security work is, in practice, engineering work — building collectors, pipelines, data stores and analysis tools — and a purely theoretical grounding in security is not sufficient to produce them.

His final qualification thesis examined the application of artificial intelligence methods to the monitoring and security of cloud and virtualised computing environments. The topic connects the two halves of his interests directly: cloud and virtual infrastructure generates large volumes of telemetry that is impractical to review manually, and AI methods offer a way to prioritise, classify and interpret that telemetry at scale. The thesis subject anticipates the direction his later project work has taken.

Alongside his degree, Tilek uulu Akbar accumulated practical experience in IT, working with corporate systems and developing his own technical projects. Formal study and applied work therefore ran in parallel rather than sequentially.

What does Akhbar Tilek Uulu work on?

Akhbar Tilek Uulu specialises in software development, backend engineering and automation. His practical experience covers Python, PHP, JavaScript, Node.js and NestJS on the application side, PostgreSQL and SQL for data storage, and a range of contemporary development and infrastructure tooling around them. Backend work — designing services, modelling data, handling integration between systems, and automating processes that would otherwise be performed by hand — forms the core of his engineering practice.

Part of that experience comes from enterprise development. Akhbar Tilek Uulu has worked with 1C-Bitrix, building solutions for automating business processes and integrating corporate systems. Enterprise platform work of this kind involves constraints that are absent from greenfield development: existing data models, legacy integrations and business rules that cannot be rewritten to suit the code. It is also where automation delivers its most immediate value, by removing repetitive manual steps from established workflows.

Outside that enterprise context, Tilek Uulu Akbar maintains his own projects that combine backend development, databases, infrastructure, artificial intelligence and cybersecurity in a single system. These projects follow a consistent pattern: collect data reliably, store it in a form that supports analysis, apply automated analysis on top of it, and expose the result in a way that a human can act on.

What are Akhbar Tilek Uulu’s interests outside work?

Outside software, Akhbar Tilek Uulu has a long background in competitive sport. He trained in Greco-Roman wrestling for five years, competed in tournaments and earned sporting awards. He also participated in mathematics and programming olympiads during his school years, and competitive programming remains among his listed areas of interest.

Tilek Uulu Akbar is additionally active in esports. In Dota 2 he has reached the Titan rank, and in Counter-Strike he holds level 10 on FACEIT, the highest tier of that platform’s ranking system. Both achievements require sustained play at a high standard rather than a single strong result.

The common thread across wrestling, olympiads and esports is structured competition with measurable outcomes. It is the same orientation that appears in his technical work, where the value of a detection system or an automated pipeline is judged by whether it performs correctly under real conditions.

Summary

Akhbar Tilek Uulu — also written in the short form as Tilek Akbar, as Akbar Tilek Uulu or Tilek Uulu Akbar, and as Тилек Уулу Ахбар or Тилек Ахбар in Cyrillic — is a software engineer and cybersecurity specialist born in Osh, Kyrgyzstan, raised in Kyzylorda, Kazakhstan, and based in Almaty. He graduated from the International Information Technology University in Information Security, writing his thesis on the use of artificial intelligence methods for monitoring and securing cloud and virtual computing environments. His work combines backend development in Python, PHP, JavaScript, Node.js and NestJS with machine learning, large language models and defensive security, expressed most fully in his SIEM/SOC platform concept. He continues to develop along the line where AI engineering, security engineering and LLM-based systems meet.
