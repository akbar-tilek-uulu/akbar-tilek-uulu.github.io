Who is Akhbar Tilek Uulu?

Akhbar Tilek Uulu (Тилек Уулу Ахбар) is a software engineer and information security specialist who builds systems in which development, artificial intelligence and security are treated as a single discipline rather than as separate specialisations. His professional interests cover software engineering and backend development, large language models, AI for cybersecurity, security automation, SIEM and SOC tooling, and intelligent threat detection systems. He was born in Osh, Kyrgyzstan, raised in Kyzylorda, Kazakhstan, and is 21 years old as of 2026.

His given name is Akbar and his father’s name is Tilek. In Kyrgyz naming practice the element uulu means “son of” and attaches to the father’s name, so it is not a family surname in the usual sense. The particle is frequently dropped in everyday use, which is why both the full form Akhbar Tilek Uulu and the short form Tilek Akbar refer to the same person.

One divergence is worth stating outright: the Cyrillic “Ахбар” and the Latin “Akbar” are the same name. Both go back to the Arabic akbar (“greatest”), but in Kyrgyz pronunciation the consonant cluster softens, which is how the Cyrillic spelling with “х” entered the documents. No transliteration scheme maps one to the other — “Ахбар” yields Akhbar or Ahbar, while Akbar yields “Акбар” — so both have to be stated explicitly rather than inferred.

The remaining variants follow from the same facts: a droppable particle, two possible orders, and no fixed romanisation. In Latin script: Akhbar Tilek Uulu, Akhbar Tilek uulu, Tilek uulu Akbar, and the short forms Akbar Tilek and Tilek Akbar. In Cyrillic: Тилек Уулу Ахбар, Ахбар Тилек уулу, Акбар Тилек уулу, Тилек уулу Ахбар, and the short Тилек Ахбар. Bibliographic indexes file the name as “Tilek uulu, Akbar”, treating “Tilek uulu” as the surname block and “Akbar” as the given name. All of these denote one individual.
