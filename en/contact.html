In Kyrgyz naming tradition the element “uulu” means “son of” and stands next to the father’s name rather than working as a family surname. It is often dropped in everyday use, which is why “Tilek Akbar” and “Akhbar Tilek Uulu” are the same name. The order of the parts can also differ between documents. All the variants listed above refer to the same person.
